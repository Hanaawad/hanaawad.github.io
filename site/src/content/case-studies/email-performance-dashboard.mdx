overview: >
  This dashboard shows the importance of the email product and its performance
  on campaigns over time. It helps measure engagement growth over time so the
  team avoids missing out on valuable insights and any significant-change alerts
  the marketing team should act on. Data is visualised according to a clear
  diagram, and the most important data is placed at the top-left corner of the
  dashboard.

problem: >
  Customers and the marketing team lacked a simple, at-a-glance view of how the
  email product was performing. They needed to get an overall feel very quickly,
  yet still dive deep into cost-per-acquisition and the KPIs that matter — without
  busy visuals pulling attention away from the data.

goals:
  - title: A simple view of performance
    body: Give customers an easy and simple view of how their Shop Email Product is performing.
  - title: Operational KPIs for marketing
    body: Provide important operational information and key performance indicators for the marketing team on cost per acquisition.
  - title: Clean, focused layout
    body: A clean layout with simple visuals — graphs and charts, not busy colours — to avoid distracting the user from the data.
  - title: An overall feel, fast
    body: Let the user get an overall feeling very quickly.
  - title: Depth on demand
    body: Let the user dive deep into the details when needed.

methods:
