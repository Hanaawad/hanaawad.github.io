About

From buildings to interfaces.

I'm Hana Awad, a passionate interface and UX designer with front end developer experience, and an ex architect from Alexandria, Egypt, now living in Copenhagen, Denmark.

Architecture taught me to think in structure and flow: how people move through a space, what they see first, and how everything fits together. I bring that same instinct to digital products, starting from research and information architecture, and carrying it through to interfaces that are clear, usable, and shipped.

As a Senior UX Design Lead, my work goes beyond screens: I set the UX direction for a product, mentor the designers on my team, and shape the quarterly roadmap, prioritising the work against user needs and business goals. I lead research and discovery and build the design systems and rituals that let a cross functional team move quickly without losing consistency. I partner with product and engineering from the first idea through to launch, and because I also write front end code, I design with the grain of what's buildable, which keeps the handoff honest and the final product close to the intent.
