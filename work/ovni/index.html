Ovni — Egypt · UX Design & Frontend development

Ovni

A platform and app that connects travellers with buyers — designed and, on the front end, built in collaboration with the design and development team.

Product site
UX Design + Frontend focus
MarketplaceMobile & webUX + Frontend

Overview

Ovni connects travellers with buyers. Working alongside the design and development team, I led the UX and also wrote front-end code — taking the concept from a brand-new idea in Egypt toward an easy-to-use proof of concept.

The problem

Buyers often want products that are only available in other countries, but it isn't always possible for them to travel and get what they want. Ovni develops an easy-to-use platform and app that connects travellers with buyers: a buyer makes a wish and adds all the details of the product they'd like, so a traveller heading to that destination can contact them and bring the product back for a delivery fee.

Project goals

Introduce mobile and tablet optimisation.

Learn for the app

Gathering insights

After collecting the recordings from the user interviews, I conducted affinity mapping with my teammate to synthesise the pains identified. We grouped these problems under common themes and features across the platform — and created personas to recognise that different people have different needs and expectations.

The key decision

Ship a web-first proof of concept and learn from real travellers and buyers before building the mobile app — so the app could be designed around evidenced pain points rather than assumptions.

Key features

Explore

Let travellers search for filtered or all wishes.

Make money

Let travellers turn a trip back to Egypt into a way to make some money.

Make a wish

Let buyers make their own wish, with a negotiated delivery fee.

Profile

Let customers view their reviews, wishes, trips, wishes to deliver and orders.

In motion

Outcome

Ovni launched as a web-first proof of concept connecting travellers and buyers, with the four core flows above — and a research base gathered from both sides to shape the mobile app that follows.
